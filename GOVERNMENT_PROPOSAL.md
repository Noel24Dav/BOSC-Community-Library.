 Formal Proposal: Adoption of BOSC Community Library
To: The Ministry of Education  
From: Lead Community Maintainer  

1. Introduction
[cite_start]This proposal advocates for adopting the BOSC Community Library over proprietary alternatives to enhance transparency and digital sovereignty in the education sector.

2. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)
[cite_start]By transitioning to this Open Source solution, the Ministry eliminates recurring licensing fees. 
Cost Savings: Funds can be redirected from software "rent" to infrastructure.
Long-term Value: The TCO is significantly lower as the software is community-maintained.

3. Avoiding Vendor Lock-in
[cite_start]Proprietary systems trap the Ministry within a single provider's ecosystem. 
Open Standards: This library uses open formats, ensuring data portability.
Freedom to Choose: The Ministry can hire any qualified IT firm for support, ensuring competitive pricing and service quality.

4. Conclusion
[cite_start]Adopting the BOSC Library ensures that public-sector data remains open, secure, and cost-effective.
